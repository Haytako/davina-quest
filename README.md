# Davina's Quest

Text RPG adventure game with real-time combat, inventory, and exploration.

> The kingdom of Eltania has fallen. The Necromancer seized the throne, and Prince Richard is hiding in a remote village. Only a true hero can defeat the darkness and reclaim the kingdom.

## Play

**[Play Davina's Quest](https://haytako.github.io/davina-quest/)**

## Features

- **4 character classes** — Warrior, Mage, Archer, Rogue — each with unique stats, skills, and attack range
- **Real-time combat** — move your character, dodge attacks, use skills with cooldowns, defeat bosses
- **9 explorable locations** — from the peaceful Starting Village to the Necromancer's Castle
- **6 challenging bosses** — Goblin King, Dark Knight, Troll Chieftain, Ghost Queen, Stone Guardian + Golem, and the final boss Necromancer
- **NPC dragon Ignis** — the last dragon, who can help you on your quest
- **Inventory & equipment** — collect weapons, potions, keys, and other items
- **Shop system** — buy and sell gear at the merchant
- **Lore system** — discover the story of Eltania through ancient obelisks scattered across the world
- **Original soundtrack** — atmospheric music throughout the journey
- **Runs in the browser** — no installation needed, works on desktop and mobile

## Locations

1. Starting Village — safe haven, shop, quest hub
2. Mill — meet the miller, begin your journey
3. Goblin Forest — beware of goblin ambushes
4. Dark Castle — cursed stronghold of the Silver Cross Order
5. Troll Lands — harsh caves and powerful chieftains
6. Haunted Cemetery — restless souls and the Ghost Queen
7. Lava Crater — fire elementals and salamanders
8. Dragon's Lair — meet Ignis, the last dragon
9. Necromancer's Castle — final showdown

## Controls

- **Click / Tap** — move character, attack enemies, interact with NPCs
- **Skills panel** — use class abilities (cost mana or stamina, have cooldowns)
- **Inventory** — manage equipment and items
- **Dialog choices** — talk to NPCs, make story decisions

## Tech

- Pure HTML5 + CSS3 + JavaScript (single file)
- Canvas-based rendering
- No frameworks or dependencies
- GitHub Pages hosting
